07 / THE DETAILS

Fair questions.
Straight answers.

What's included in the starting price?＋

The listed deliverables for the package as confirmed in a written scope. Hosting, domains, paid tools, extra pages, copywriting, and third-party costs are separate unless included.

How long does a website take?＋

It depends on scope, content readiness, and feedback. We'll agree on realistic milestones before work begins.

Can you redesign my existing site?＋

Yes. We can review the current experience, identify friction, and plan a clearer visual direction and build.

Do you make 3D websites and animations?＋

Yes, when motion supports the idea. Complex 3D, custom illustrations, and advanced interactions are scoped separately and tested for performance.

How do payments work?＋

Payment milestones are agreed in the proposal before work begins. We start after scope and initial payment terms are accepted.

Can I see client case studies?＋

The portfolio includes clearly labelled concept studies and independent builds. We don't present concept work as client work or invent testimonials.
