05 / FAIR QUESTIONS

Before we
get started.

Can you really build a website under ₹10,000? ＋

Yes, for a clearly scoped one-page website or landing page. Custom applications, e-commerce, extensive copywriting, and paid third-party services are not included in the Launch package.

Is the domain and hosting included? ＋

They are separate unless your written proposal explicitly says otherwise. We'll explain the options and recurring costs before you commit.

How long will my website take? ＋

Small, ready-to-build projects may take around one to two weeks after content and requirements are confirmed. Larger projects take longer. We'll provide a realistic timeline in your proposal.

Can you add features later? ＋

Usually, yes. We'll plan the first version around your current needs and quote future features separately when you're ready.

How do payments work? ＋

Payment milestones will be written into the proposal before work starts. We don't begin a project until scope, timeline, and payment terms are agreed.
